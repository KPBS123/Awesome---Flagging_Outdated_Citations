# Datasets

## 1. arXiv Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle / Cornell University arXiv

**Description:**
The arXiv dataset contains a large collection of scientific research papers and metadata, including paper titles, authors, abstracts, categories, and publication information.

**Application to the Project:**
This dataset can be used to analyze research-paper publication dates, compare older and newer papers, and identify newer papers that may be relevant alternatives to older citations.

**Link:**
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/Cornell-University/arxiv

## 2. Research Papers Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

**Description:**
This dataset contains information about research papers, including metadata such as titles, authors, publication years, abstracts, citations, and references.

**Application to the Project:**
The dataset can be used to study citation relationships and publication age. It can help identify potentially outdated references and find newer research connected to the same topic.

**Link:**
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nechbamohammed/research-papers-dataset

## 3. Massive Scholarly Dataset: 5M Papers and 36M Citations

**Source:** Kaggle

**Description:**
This dataset contains millions of scholarly papers and citation relationships. It can be used for large-scale analysis of scientific literature and citation networks.

**Application to the Project:**
The dataset is useful for analyzing citation networks, citation age, research influence, and relationships between older and newer scientific papers.

**Link:**
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/agungpambudi/research-citation-network-5m-papers

## Dataset Usage Note

The original datasets may be very large. Therefore, only small relevant samples should be uploaded to this repository when necessary. The original datasets remain available through their respective source links.

The datasets will be used only for research and educational purposes and will not be redistributed beyond their permitted usage.
